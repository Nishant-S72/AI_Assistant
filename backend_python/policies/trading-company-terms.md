# Global Trading Company - Terms and Conditions

## 1. Company Overview

Global Trading Company (GTC) is an international trading firm specializing in the import and export of consumer goods, industrial equipment, and raw materials. Established in 2010, we operate across 45 countries and maintain strategic partnerships with manufacturers, distributors, and logistics providers worldwide.

Our mission is to facilitate seamless international trade while ensuring compliance with all applicable regulations, maintaining the highest standards of quality, and providing exceptional customer service. We are committed to ethical business practices, environmental sustainability, and fostering long-term relationships with our clients.

### 1.1 Business Scope

GTC engages in the following activities:
- Import and export of consumer goods including electronics, textiles, and household items
- Trading of industrial equipment and machinery
- Sourcing and distribution of raw materials
- Supply chain management and logistics coordination
- Trade financing and payment facilitation
- Customs clearance and documentation services

### 1.2 Regulatory Compliance

All transactions conducted through GTC are subject to compliance with:
- International trade regulations (WTO, bilateral trade agreements)
- Export control laws and sanctions
- Customs and import/export regulations
- Product safety and quality standards
- Environmental protection requirements
- Anti-corruption and anti-bribery laws

## 5. Shipping and Delivery

### 5.1 Shipping Methods

We offer various shipping methods based on product type, destination, and urgency:
- **Sea Freight**: FCL (Full Container Load) and LCL (Less than Container Load)
- **Air Freight**: Express and standard air cargo
- **Land Transport**: Road and rail freight for regional deliveries
- **Courier Services**: DHL, FedEx, UPS for small parcels

Shipping method recommendations are provided based on cost, speed, and reliability requirements. Final shipping method is subject to client approval.

### 5.2 Incoterms

We operate under standard International Commercial Terms (Incoterms 2020):
- **EXW (Ex Works)**: Buyer responsible for all costs and risks from seller's premises
- **FOB (Free On Board)**: Seller delivers goods on board vessel at named port
- **CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight)**: Seller pays costs and freight to named destination port
- **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)**: Seller delivers goods to buyer's location with all duties paid

Incoterms are specified in each sales contract. Risk of loss transfers according to the agreed Incoterm.

### 5.3 Delivery Times
