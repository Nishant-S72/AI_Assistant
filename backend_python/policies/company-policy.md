# Company Policy Document

## Overview
Our company offers a comprehensive refund policy to ensure customer satisfaction. All refund requests are processed in accordance with the following guidelines.

## Refund Policy

### Eligibility
Customers are eligible for refunds within 30 days of purchase. The product must be in its original condition and packaging.

### Process
To request a refund, customers must contact our support team with their order number and reason for return. Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

### Exceptions
Digital products and services are non-refundable once delivered. Custom orders cannot be refunded unless there is a manufacturing defect.

## Return Policy

### Timeframe
Returns are accepted within 30 days of purchase for physical products.

### Condition Requirements
Items must be unused, in original packaging, and include all accessories.

## Shipping Policy

### Standard Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free for orders over $50.

### Express Shipping
Express shipping is available for an additional fee and delivers within 2-3 business days.

## Privacy Policy

### Data Collection
We collect only necessary information to process orders and provide customer service.

### Data Usage
Customer data is used solely for order processing and will not be shared with third parties without consent.

## Terms of Service

### User Responsibilities
Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their account credentials.

### Company Rights
We reserve the right to modify these terms at any time. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of updated terms.
